# EcoSmart – AI-Based Waste Management System
Group: ECO TECH AI COLLECTIVE

## Problem Statement
Improper waste segregation leads to pollution and low recycling.

## Solution
AI system to detect waste and guide recycling.

## Functional Requirements
- Upload images
- AI detection
- Show waste type
- Recycling tips

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Secure
- Fast
- Scalable

## Users
Citizens and municipalities
